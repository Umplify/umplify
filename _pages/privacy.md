**Last updated:** August 16, 2026

Umplify Technologies Inc. ("Umplify", "we", "us") is based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

When you submit a form on this site, we collect the information you type into it. Depending on the form, that can include:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your phone number
- Your business name, industry, approximate size, and general location
- Anything you write in the free-text fields, such as a description of your current challenges

We also collect standard website analytics through Google Analytics, including pages visited and approximate location derived from your IP address. We have IP anonymization enabled.

## Why we collect it

We use the information you submit for one purpose: to respond to your enquiry and to have a conversation about whether we can help your business. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

## Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored in Microsoft Azure in the Canada Central region, which is physically located in Ontario. Email notifications are delivered through Resend, our email provider. Website analytics are processed by Google.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for two years from your last contact with us, then delete them. If you ask us to delete your information sooner, we will do that.

## Consent and unsubscribing

We only send you commercial email if you gave us consent on a form, or if we have an existing business relationship with you. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can also just reply and ask us to stop. We will action it.

## Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. Email [arash.sabet@umplify.com](mailto:arash.sabet@umplify.com) and we will respond within thirty days.

## Cookies

This site uses cookies for Google Analytics. You can block cookies in your browser settings without losing access to anything on this site.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be described here rather than made quietly.
